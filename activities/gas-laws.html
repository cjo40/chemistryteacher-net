How to use this lab
  1. Pick a law at the top. The green boxes tell you what is held constant.
  2. Each law lets you change either of its two variables. Use the buttons on the model (Push / Pull, Add / Remove weight, Raise / Lower, Heat / Cool) or the Controls box. The other variable responds.
  3. Record at least 3 trials at different values. 5 or more makes a better graph.
  4. Graph your data. Is it a straight line or a curve? For Boyle's law, try graphing P against 1/V.
  5. Find absolute zero: in Charles's or Gay-Lussac's law, graph against T (°C), turn on the best-fit line and Extend line to y = 0.
  6. Turn on the "constant?" column. Which combination stays the same? Then switch the divisor to °C. What goes wrong?
  7. Answer the challenge questions. Test each answer in the model.

Model: ideal gas, 0.00204 mol (50.0 mL at 25 °C and 101.3 kPa). The syringe holds 5 – 120 mL. Each weight adds 25 kPa. Data is saved in this browser only. Export it to keep it.
