Going deeper: Careful experiments (J. Chem. Educ. 2000) show the stream bends mainly because the charged balloon induces a net charge on the droplets as they break off — charge can move through polar liquids like water, acetone and ethanol, but not through nonpolar hexane. Molecules simply rotating their dipoles would give only a tiny pull. Either way, the classroom result is the same: polar liquid streams bend toward a charged object; nonpolar ones do not.
