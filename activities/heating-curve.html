Teacher settings
Heating: ice at −50 °C → steam at 150 °C. Cooling: steam at 150 °C → ice at −50 °C.
Each full run takes 30 real seconds and the clock shows 0:00 → 10:00. Switching heating/cooling starts a new run.
The flat parts are at 0 °C (melting) and 100 °C (boiling).
Lab view: real time-lapse footage of one run, ice → melting → boiling → dry, synced to the graph. V = show/hide.
