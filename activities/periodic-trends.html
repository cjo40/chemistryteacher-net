Atomic radius · Ionization energy · Electron affinity · Electronegativity

    Data sources & notes
    • Atomic radius: calculated atomic radii (pm), E. Clementi, D. L. Raimondi & W. P. Reinhardt, J. Chem. Phys. 38, 2686 (1963) and 47, 1300 (1967), as tabulated by WebElements (M. Winter, University of Sheffield). One radius type is used everywhere so noble gases can be compared.
    • First ionization energy: NIST Atomic Spectra Database / CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics values in kJ/mol (Tc: Mattolat et al., 2010).
    • Electron affinity: experimental gas-phase values (kJ/mol) from laser photodetachment studies (J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data compilations and later measurements), as compiled in Wikipedia’s “Electron affinity (data page)”, Nov 2026. Sign convention: positive = energy released. n/a = anion not stable. * = estimate (Tc, Po).
    • Electronegativity: Pauling scale (revised values, Allred 1961) as compiled by WebElements. Noble gases are not given values in this activity.
    • Lanthanides (Z = 57–71) are kept in the embedded data but not used in Practice.
    • Ionic radii (Learn stop 7 and quiz): R. D. Shannon, Acta Cryst. A32, 751 (1976), 6-coordinate.
